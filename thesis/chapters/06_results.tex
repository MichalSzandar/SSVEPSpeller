\subsection{Spelled sentences}
\label{sec:spelled-sentences}

\Cref{tab:spelled-sentences} lists every sentence I spelled online, in the order I
spelled them. The two phrases of 16 November come from the phrase set
of~\citet{mackenzie2003phrase}; on that day I stopped after two of the three planned
phrases. I chose the three phrases of 25 November myself and copied them twice, first
with distilgpt2 suggestions and then without suggestions, so the two conditions can be
compared phrase by phrase. After the first of these runs I also spelled a sentence of my
own in free spelling. Free spelling has no ground truth, so it has no accuracy, and the
sentence is given as I left it, with one wrong letter.
